Arden Sheets

Arden Sheets is a private tool used only by Anselm (Jay) Okojie, director of Rosebud Global, to let his own operations assistant read and update Google Sheets in his own Google account.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not sold as a service.

What it does: reads and writes cells in spreadsheets that the account owner already has access to (for example, a task sheet shared with his own ChatGPT workspace). It requests only the Google Sheets permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets).
